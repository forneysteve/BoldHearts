For Teams

Leadership Team Culture

A structured process for church and organizational leadership teams who want an honest picture of their culture — and a path toward something healthier.

We administer the Leadership Health Assessment to your full team, aggregate the results into a team culture report, meet individually with team members, and facilitate a 90-minute debrief conversation that names what’s working, what’s at risk, and what needs to change. The goal isn’t a better culture deck — it’s a leadership team that actually knows each other, trusts each other, and can sustain the work they’re called to.

Best for

  • Pastoral & elder teams
  • Nonprofit leadership
  • Ministry directors
  • Church staff teams

Format

Assessment · Team Culture Report · Individual sessions · 90-min debrief

Fees & Accessibility

I work with a range of financial situations and don’t want cost to be the barrier that keeps you from the care you need.

  • Standard Rate — for those with stable income and resources
  • Sliding Scale — for those in transition or with limited margin
  • Scholarship / Pay-What-You-Can — for those in significant financial strain

No one is turned away for inability to pay.

How Sessions Work

  • Silence, prayer, and contemplative practices may be used as appropriate.
  • You set the pace; I accompany you.
  • We regularly check in on how the work is landing for you.
